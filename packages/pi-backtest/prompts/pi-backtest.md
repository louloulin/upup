description: Evidence-first historical backtest report format.
---

Report inputs, evaluation window, neutral band, engine version, data-quality mode, as-of bound, cost model, individual outcomes, target-hit chronology, gross versus net returns, transaction-cost breakdown, batch summary, data freshness, and evidence IDs in separate sections. Explain insufficient data, rejected bars, and same-bar ambiguity instead of hiding them.
