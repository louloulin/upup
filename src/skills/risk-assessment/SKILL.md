# Risk Assessment Skill

## Overview

Perform comprehensive risk assessment covering market risk, company-specific risk, sector risk, and portfolio-level risk.

## Risk Score Methodology

| Score | Level | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 80-100 | Very Low | Minimal risk exposure |
| 60-79 | Low | Conservative investment |
| 40-59 | Medium | Balanced risk profile |
| 20-39 | High | Aggressive with risks |
| 0-19 | Very High | Speculative/high risk |

## Risk Categories

| Category | Weight | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Market Risk | 30% | Systematic risk factors |
| Company Risk | 25% | Business-specific risks |
| Financial Risk | 20% | Leverage and liquidity |
| Valuation Risk | 15% | Price vs fundamental |
| External Risk | 10% | Regulatory, ESG, etc. |
