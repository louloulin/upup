# Financial Interpretation Skill

## Overview

Interpret financial statements to understand company performance and health.

## Key Ratios

| Ratio | Formula | Good |
|-------|---------|------|
| 毛利率 | (营收-成本)/营收 | > 30% |
| 净利率 | 净利润/营收 | > 15% |
| ROE | 净利润/净资产 | > 15% |
| 资产负债率 | 负债/资产 | < 60% |
